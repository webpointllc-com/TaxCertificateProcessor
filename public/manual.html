FAQ

Short answers for title, tax-service, and mortgage desks.

What does the as-of date mean?

Each certificate card shows as of the date the draft was generated. Taxes move. Treat the card as a dated snapshot and verify balances on the collector portal before closing. If figures change, run a fresh batch for an updated as-of.

Chippewa portal says Guest Sign In. Is that correct?

Yes. The locked Chippewa tax-search page is the LandNav public portal. Use Guest Sign In, then search. Do not use a dead or guessed county host. The official lock is https://pp-chippewa-co-wi-fb.app.landnav.com/login/index/

Local treasurer or county treasurer?

In Chippewa County, current December/January installments are paid to the local municipal treasurer. The county treasurer collects postponed and delinquent real estate tax. The LandNav portal is the public search; the county Treasurer office page is https://chippewacountywi.gov/169/Treasurer

How do updates work?

Drafts are point-in-time. For an update, keep the same file number when you can, process a new batch, and read the new as-of date on each card. Always re-open the collector page before you rely on the figures.

What does “verified” mean on S-PUL?

A verified badge means the tax-search URL is locked from WebPoint’s jurisdiction list as the official page for that collector — not a search-engine guess. pattern_matched is a stored pattern that still needs human confirmation. not_found means there is no locked URL; do not invent one.

Why is there a 10-parcel limit?

Chippewa’s LandNav payment cart allows 10 parcels per transaction. WebPoint’s members demo uses the same cap. Split the closing into another batch if you have more than ten.
